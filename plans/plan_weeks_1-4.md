## Side plan: home strength and mobility (optional, logged lightly)

Not part of the main plan. Two short sessions a week (about 20 min) plus 5-10 min of mobility on most days.

- Placement: after an interval ride on the same day, or on a rest day that is not before a check, a test or the 100 km. Core and upper body only on the day before an interval or check day. Week 2: Mon 16 core and upper body only (Tue is the baseline check), Thu 19 the full routine at 1-2 sets after the ride. No leg work on 27/28 Nov (100 km) or Thu 3 Dec (20-min test on Sat).
- Lower body: box squat to a chair (then bodyweight squat, then slow or with a loaded backpack); split squat (then Bulgarian split squat with the rear foot on a chair); glute bridge (then single-leg); single-leg hip hinge with a backpack, one hand on a chair; calf raises on a stair (then single-leg).
- Upper body and core: incline push-up on a table or bed edge (then the floor); backpack row; plank; side plank; dead bug; bird dog.
- Dose: 2 sets of 8-12 reps (holds 20-40 s), stop 2-3 reps before failure, slow and controlled, no pain. Move to the harder variant after two sessions at the top of the range. The first week at 1 set to limit soreness.
- Mobility: hip-flexor or couch stretch, hamstring stretch, figure-4 glute stretch, open-book thoracic rotation, cat-cow, calf stretch with a straight and a bent knee (30-45 s each, or 8 slow reps).
- Log: date, done (or sets), soreness the next day 0-10.
