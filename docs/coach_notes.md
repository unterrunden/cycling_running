## 9. Lessons that still change behaviour
1. 4DP order is sprints, 5 min, 20 min, then 1 min (my first protocol was wrong).
2. Never assume a configured value (FTP settings were 150/157 W, now 170): read it.
3. Check HR files for flat-lines and strap artefacts before any HR analysis; treat the HR-flag counter in analysis_tools.py as uninformative for rides (use `hr_plateaus` / `hrv_quality`).
4. Verify sessions against Strava (Pat forgets to mention some).
5. Calibrate expectations with data: my first 5K estimate (25:30-26:30) was far too slow, and my 4DP targets were exceeded in every effort.
6. Respect his warm-up habit (short jog, not 12-15 min); pacing per km only.
7. Time the 5.0 km mark on loop courses; do not subtract distance corrections he already accounted for.
8. Sunset and daylight times: compute (`cp.sun_times`), do not quote from memory.
9. Map ride GPS onto the route before judging effort positions (1 Nov: the 1 min was ridden on an out-and-back, not where the ride's km suggested).
10. Take metadata from the source (I put Flammgasse in Brühl from memory; Strava says Bornheim) and align recordings by HR, not by stated start times.
11. HR lags 30-90 s: a short hard effort can average 130-140 bpm and be genuine; check the HR at its end before calling it an artefact.
12. HR is inconsistent between sessions [Pat 10 Nov: occasional outliers that do not capture the real intensity]. Data agree: day-to-day spread about +-6 bpm at the same power; 10 Nov easy run about +10-15 bpm at 6:00/km. Never pace or plan by HR alone: power leads on the bike, pace and feel on easy runs; HR caps are soft upper guides; judge HR by trends over several comparable sessions, and use it to cross-check.
13. Strava activity summaries can be wrong after strap trouble: its average HR includes flat-lined missing data and GPS re-fixes after stops inflate max speed and short-stretch pace; read the stream before quoting a summary value.
14. Running the cloned repo scripts may need Pat's explicit confirmation in the chat (the session's permission check); he confirmed on 10 Nov.
15. Edit Project docs from a local copy, never by retyping them from a `project_read` result (which comes back as text and may be long); without a local copy ask Pat to attach the doc.
16. Align KICKR (MyWhoosh) and Edge recordings by power, not HR: MyWhoosh's HR arrives about 4 s late (11 Nov). Compare meters on the same stretch (block averages), not by bands binned on one meter when the other holds ERG targets.
17. Read the lap table and the trainer channels before interpreting an odd effort, then ask Pat: on 11 Nov a "20-s sprint" at 324 W and 130-160 rpm was a pulled-out power cable, not a weak effort.
18. A Garmin copy of an indoor ride that stays on Strava as 'Ride' enters the outdoor best efforts (11 Nov: 15 s / 30 s): check the connector's Ride bests after indoor sessions.
