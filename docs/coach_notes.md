### HRR, decoupling and EF
- HRR (1-min HR recovery) [Pat 20 Nov spec; Pat 21 Nov: the tightened rule agreed]:
  - Effort end = 20 s at 90%+ FTP (runs faster than 5:30/km), then 60 s of real recovery (at most 65% FTP and every 10-s mean at most 75% FTP, i.e. rolling or standing; runs slower than 7:30/km); drop from the peak (within -5..+15 s of the end) to the mean at +58..62 s.
  - Only peaks from Z3 (ride 149, run 164); max = peak in Z5 (ride 178, run 188), mod = Z3-Z4. The 60 s must be smooth and slow down (two late-collapsing recoveries on 16 and 23 Sep were strap artefacts; rule tightened).
  - So far: mod median 25.6 bpm (19 recoveries, 10-40), max median 35.8 bpm (25 recoveries, 27-49). Cells 'mod; max' with avg, best. 21 Nov: no qualifying recovery (no hard effort followed by a real recovery); 22 Nov: mod average 25, best 34 bpm.
- Decoupling: first half vs second half (NP/HR, runs speed/HR) without the first 10 min (runs 5) and the last 5 (runs 2); best = closest to 0; flags i (intervals or hard: IF above 0.90, 30-s power above 105% FTP for over 5% of the time, or half NPs differing by over 8%), s (short), u (not enough data). Most September rides are 'i'; meaningful values so far only on the easy rides (25 Sep +0.2, 16 Oct +4.2, 14 Nov -3.4, 17 Nov +0.1, 22 Nov -4.1).
  - [mine] A negative value means HR fell relative to power in the second half: on 22 Nov the colder late afternoon and the long stops can do that, so read it as 'no drift', not as a gain.
- EF: NP / average HR (runs m/min / HR); outdoor rides median about 1.08, indoor about 1.02 (fan, meter, room): compare like with like.
